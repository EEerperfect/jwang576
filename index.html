01 / About

Building tools for more precise medicine.

My work sits at the intersection of materials science, magnetic physics, and cancer biology. I design high-performance nanomaterials and pair them with advanced imaging to study how nanoparticles move, activate, and interact with biological barriers inside tumors.

The long-term aim is translational: to turn mechanistic understanding into theranostic platforms that can improve both diagnosis and treatment.

02 / Research

Three scales, one question

How can we engineer nanomaterials that respond intelligently to disease—and watch them work in living systems?

01

Magnetic nanomaterials

Designing nanoparticle composition, structure, and magnetic anisotropy for MRI, hyperthermia, and biosensing.

  • Particle synthesis
  • Magnetic characterization
  • Structure–property relationships

02

Activatable theranostics

Developing enzyme-responsive nanoparticles that sharpen tumor specificity, penetration, and therapeutic action.

  • Tumor microenvironment
  • Brain cancer
  • Responsive delivery

03

Multimodal imaging

Combining MRI, magnetic particle imaging, and optical imaging to quantify nano–bio interactions in vivo.

  • MRI & MPI
  • Optical imaging
  • In vivo translation
